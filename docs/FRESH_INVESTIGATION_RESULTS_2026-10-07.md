# Fresh constructed investigations, October 7

Complete reviewed inputs, outputs, synthetic sources and runtime evidence for the October 7 studies are now in [the offline verification export](OCTOBER7_EVIDENCE_VERIFICATION.md). Original private evidence remains unchanged; path redactions and omitted human material are declared.

The V8 larger-resource experiment completed 168 requests without retries: 24 analyst preparations, 72 investigator answers and 72 supplementary different-model reviews. There are 18 distinct measured cases, each investigated under three conditions, plus six practice cases also investigated under each condition. Practice is excluded from the measured denominator.

| Measured condition | Oracle accepted / 18 |
| --- | ---: |
| A: evidence and deterministic context catalogue | 18 |
| B: same evidence plus model-selected analyst assistance | 17 |
| C: assistance with exact repetitions collapsed | 17 |

The total is 52/54 measured answers. All 18 practice investigations passed. The cases cover empty windows with complete or incomplete comparison, STUN repetition, UID fixtures, mixed blocks and changing activity. Their 24 original source hashes are distinct and were sealed before execution. The author knows the labels, the scenario families repeat and the system receives explicit catalogues and output instructions. These are new constructed source bytes, not unseen real incidents or 54 independent incident cases.

All roles requested 8,192 context, 2,048 output tokens, 16 CPU threads and GPU offload on the owned Vulkan endpoint, with a 300-second deadline and thinking disabled. Analyst/investigator used Qwen3.5 4B; supplementary review used Qwen3.5 9B. Every response stopped naturally. The final audit regenerated all exported jobs, rechecked exact request/response and combined-channel hashes, reassessed full-input runtime traces, verified the observed installed digest/GPU allocation and idle release, and recomputed the investigation and reviewer grades.

Both measured failures are B and C on the third pack's mixed blocked/UID/STUN case. The group contains the original reference `E-0c8ea6fcd3be1efb`; both answers returned `E-0c8ea61cd3be1efb`. Counts, actions and uncertainty were otherwise correct, but one changed character breaks the citation. The exact-source oracle rejected both. The 9B reviewer missed both errors. No answer was repaired or retried.

The reviewer matched the full source-backed checklist on 49/72 reviews, including practice. Its disagreements include false alarms about required next checks and decisions, as well as both missed bad citations. A model review cannot override the original-record checks. Median measured request times were 8.953, 9.235 and 9.258 seconds for A, B and C, excluding analyst preparation and supplementary review. These are inference times, not human investigation times.

The orchestration failed after finishing pack 2 because the frozen runner tried to recreate the immutable first `progress.json`. All 112 completed request receipts were verified before continuation. An additive, pre-request-sealed wrapper executed only the never-requested third pack with the same frozen prompts, models, settings and source bytes. The original failure and outputs remain unchanged. The working runner now uses distinct immutable checkpoints, with a regression covering all three batches.

Candidate pack 04 was unused and excluded: its empty cases overlapped an earlier pack. The corrected, sealed `fresh-cases-05` pack supplied this experiment. The original batch protocol's legacy `requested_options` field contains values before role overrides; the actual requests, role-options file and execution seal bind the V8 settings. The old metadata is preserved rather than rewritten.

During execution, ordinary model reporting and its recovery checker were paused while collection, SQLite indexing, the snapshot dashboard and native health sampling continued. Recovery returned ready at 18:36:54 UTC. Subsequent ordinary reports completed and passed their source-fact checker. The receiver was not stopped for the continuation.

This experiment demonstrates execution and source-bound decision checks on these constructed cases. It does not show improved human accuracy, new threat detection or successful follow-up investigation. Suggested next checks were not executed. The two assisted failures do not establish that assistance causes errors; one case and one output per condition cannot support that causal conclusion.

## First actual operator pilot

Trevor subsequently completed the practice and first six-case session in the sealed local interface. Fourteen immutable initial/final answer receipts passed verification. Practice is excluded; all six measured cases have valid supporting references and all three supplied blocks were identified and cited. Original answers remain unchanged, with agent grades recorded separately under the frozen rubric.

The useful weakness was interpreting allowed sensor events as successful communication. The supplied cases contain no client/server receipt proving success. Participant feedback also found repeated STUN lists tedious, including the grouped case. Subsequent cases used a generic renderer rather than the dedicated first-case format; that deviation was preserved during the session. Browser timings can include idle time, and two first-evidence timings are missing rather than zero.

One operator with two measured cases per condition does not establish a causal improvement in accuracy, effort or speed. The prepared second and third sessions remain unexecuted. They are later study work, not invented participants. The first session and its source-backed assessment are complete.
