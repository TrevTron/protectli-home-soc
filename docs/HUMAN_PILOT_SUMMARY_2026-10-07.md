# First operator pilot

One actual operator completed practice and six measured constructed cases in the sealed local interface. Fourteen locked initial/final receipts and the pre-session display, source and rubric seals passed verification. Practice and QA remain excluded. All six measured cases cited valid original records, and all three supplied blocks were identified and cited.

The source-backed agent assessment found incomplete group counts and sensor allowance interpreted as successful communication without a client/server receipt. Participant feedback found the repeated STUN lists tedious, including the grouped case. No next checks were executed. Original answers are unchanged and remain private; this candidate does not distribute the participant journal, raw responses or browser timing records.

This was an operator pilot, not independent participant authentication, an exam or a causal study of analyst benefit. Each condition had two measured cases; repeated scenario families, order, familiarity, missing first-evidence timings and browser idle time limit interpretation. Later cases used a generic renderer rather than the dedicated first-case layout. That display deviation was preserved during the session and needs correction and a new seal before another controlled session.
